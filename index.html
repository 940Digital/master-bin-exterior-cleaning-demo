Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How often should I have my trash bins cleaned?

We recommend having your bins cleaned every six weeks. Quarterly (every 12 weeks) and one-time cleanings are also available.

How does your bin cleaning process work?

Every bin is power washed and sanitized with water heated to 200°F, killing 99.9% of bacteria, then hand-pressure-washed on the exterior and deodorized.

What areas do you cover?

Frisco, McKinney, Allen, Celina, Plano, and Prosper, TX.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. There are no long-term contracts on any plan, and you can cancel anytime.
